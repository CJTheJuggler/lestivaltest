About Us

Lestival: The Leicester Circus Festival is a one day juggling convention, first held in 2008 in our cavenous venue: Brockington College, Leicester. Through the years as we've evolved a few things have remained constant, great workshops, festival vibes and an amazing show, whether you're a beginner juggler or an experienced circus professional there is something for all at Lestival
