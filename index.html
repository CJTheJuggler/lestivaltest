About Us

Lestival is a one-day circus gathering all about connection, creativity, and shared joy. Throughout the day, people dip into workshops, pick up new skills, and catch up with familiar faces.

When evening rolls in, everyone comes together for a vibrant show that celebrates the performers who bring our community to life.
